## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have the following:

<Callout kind="info">
- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- A valid email address for account verification
- Optional: Existing project documents in PDF, Markdown, or DOCX format
</Callout>

## Step 1: Sign Up and Log In

Create your Newby Enterprises account in under 2 minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Dashboard" icon="monitor">
    Navigate to `https://dashboard.example.com` and click **Sign Up**.

    Select **Continue with Email** or use your preferred provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email" icon="mail">
    Enter your email and create a strong password. Check your inbox for the verification link and click it to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Return to the dashboard, enter your credentials, and access your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Create Your First Documentation Space

Organize your projects by creating dedicated spaces.

<Steps>
  <Step title="New Space" icon="plus">
    From the dashboard, click **New Space**. Enter a name like `Project Alpha Docs` and select a template (e.g., "Team Documentation").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize Settings" icon="settings">
    Add a description, set permissions for team members, and choose a workspace color.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Enter" icon="arrow-right">
    Click **Create**. You'll land in your new space ready for content.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Upload Your First Documents

Add content quickly via multiple methods.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Drag & Drop" icon="upload">
    Open your space and drag files directly into the editor canvas.

    Supported formats: PDF, Markdown (`*.md`), Word (`*.docx`), images.

    Files auto-organize into pages.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Bulk Upload" icon="package">
    Click **Upload** in the sidebar.

    Select multiple files from your computer. Metadata like titles extract automatically.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API Integration" icon="code">
    For automation, use the Spaces API.

    <CodeGroup tabs="cURL,JavaScript">
      ```bash
      curl -X POST https://api.example.com/v1/spaces/{spaceId}/documents \
        -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
        -F "file=@/path/to/document.md"
      ```
      ```javascript
      const formData = new FormData();
      formData.append('file', documentFile);

      await fetch(`https://api.example.com/v1/spaces/{spaceId}/documents`, {
        method: 'POST',
        headers: { 'Authorization': 'Bearer YOUR_API_KEY' },
        body: formData
      });
      ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
Pro tip: Use the `{spaceId}` from your space settings for API calls. Find it under **Space Settings > API**.
</Callout>
