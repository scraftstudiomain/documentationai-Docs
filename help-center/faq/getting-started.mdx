## Overview

Starting a project is easy with Newby Enteprises.

## Steps

1. Sign in to your dashboard at `https://dashboard.newbyenteprises.com`
2. Click "New Project" on the home screen
3. Enter your project name and description
4. Click "Create"

## Next Steps

- Add documentation pages to your project
- Invite collaborators using their email addresses
