## Overview

Start organizing your documentation with a new project.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Go to `https://dashboard.newbyenteprises.com` and log in with your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start new project">
    Click on "New Project" in the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter details">
    Fill in your project name and optional description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Click "Create". Your new project dashboard has been set up!
  </Step>
</Steps>
